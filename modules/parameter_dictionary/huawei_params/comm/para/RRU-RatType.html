Meaning

Indicates the standards that are supported by the RRU or RFU. The value NULL indicates that obtaining the supported modes fails or the board does not support the query of supported modes. The parameter values consist of different RATs. LO, LF, and L indicate LTE-FDD, T and TD indicate LTE-TDD, W and WO indicate WiMAX, C and CO indicate CDMA, G and GO indicate GSM, U and UO indicate UMTS, R and RO indicate RFA, M and MO indicate NB-IoT, NF indicates NR-FDD, and NTD indicates NR-TDD.
