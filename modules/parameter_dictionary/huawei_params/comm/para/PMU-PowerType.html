Parameter Relationship

-Configuration constraints:

  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]In a PMU MO, when PTYPE is set to SC48200, BCV in the BATTERY MO corresponding to the PMU MO must be smaller than or equal to DCVUTHD in the PMU MO minus 15.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]In a PMU MO, when PTYPE is set to a value other than SC48200, BCV in the BATTERY MO corresponding to the PMU MO must be smaller than DCVUTHD in the PMU MO minus 10.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]LSDV in the PMU MO must be greater than or equal to SDV in the BATTERY MO plus 5 under the following conditions: 1. PTYPE in the PMU MO is set to SC48200; 2. LSDF in the PMU MO is set to ENABLE; 3. LVSDF in the BATTERY MO corresponding to the PMU MO is also set to ENABLE.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]In a PMU MO, when PTYPE is set to a value other than SC48200, FCV in the BATTERY MO corresponding to the PMU MO must be greater than DCVLTHD in the PMU MO plus 20.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]SDV in the BATTERY MO corresponding to the PMU MO must be smaller than DCVLTHD in the PMU under the following conditions: 1. LSDF in the PMU MO is set to DISABLE; 2. LVSDF in the BATTERY MO corresponding to the PMU MO is set to ENABLE; 3. PTYPE in the PMU MO is set to a value other than SC48200.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]SDV in the BATTERY MO corresponding to the PMU MO must be smaller than LSDV in the PMU under the following conditions: 1. LSDF in the PMU MO is set to ENABLE; 2. LVSDF in the BATTERY MO corresponding to the PMU MO is set to ENABLE; 3. PTYPE in the PMU MO is set to a value other than SC48200.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to a value other than SC48200, INSTALLTYPE in the BATTERY MO must be set to INNER or OUTER.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to SC48200, INSTALLTYPE in the BATTERY MO must be set to OUTER.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to a value other than SC48200, and BN in the BATTERY MO is 1, BC1 in the BATTERY MO must be in the range from 5 to 5000; If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to a value other than SC48200, and BN in the BATTERY MO is 2, BC1 and BC2 in the BATTERY MO must be in the range from 5 to 5000.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to a value other than SC48200, BCV in the BATTERY MO must be in the range from 433 to 576.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to SC48200, BCV in the BATTERY MO must be in the range from 542 to 575.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to a value other than SC48200, FCV in the BATTERY MO must be in the range from 432 to 575.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to SC48200, FCV in the BATTERY MO must be in the range from 520 to 563.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to a value other than SC48200, BCV in the BATTERY MO must be greater than FCV.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to SC48200, BCV in the BATTERY MO must be greater than or equal to the value of FCV plus 22.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to a value other than SC48200, TCC in the BATTERY MO must be smaller than or equal to 500 mV/degree Celsius (278 mV/degree Fahrenheit).
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to SC48200, TCC in the BATTERY MO must be smaller than or equal to 100 mV/degree Celsius (56 mV/degree Fahrenheit).
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to a value other than SC48200, TUTHD in the BATTERY MO must be in the range from 35 to 80 degree Celsius (39 to 176 degree Fahrenheit).
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to SC48200, TUTHD in the BATTERY MO must be in the range from 35 to 60 degree Celsius (95 to 140 degree Fahrenheit).
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to a value other than SC48200, SDT in the BATTERY MO must be greater than or equal to 40 degree Celsius (104 degree Fahrenheit) and smaller than or equal to 70 degree Celsius (158 degree Fahrenheit).
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to a value other than SC48200, SDV in the BATTERY MO must be in the range from 350 to 553.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to SC48200, BC1 in the BATTERY MO must be in the range from 50 to 5000.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]When PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to SC48200 and HTSDF in the BATTERY MO is set to ENABLE, SDT in the BATTERY MO must be in the range from 45 to 70 degree Celsius (113 to 158 degree Fahrenheit).
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]When PTYPE in the upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO is set to SC48200 and LVSDF in the BATTERY MO is set to ENABLE, SDV in the BATTERY MO must be in the range from 450 to 480.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]When the values of LSDF in the PMU MO and LVSDF in the BATTERY MO are set to ENABLE, SDV in the BATTERY MO corresponding to the PMU MO with PTYPE set to a value other than SC48200 must be smaller than LSDV in the PMU MO.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]In a PMU MO, when PTYPE is set to EPU, the value of SRN must be equal to 17 or 18. When the value of PTYPE is not set to EPU, the value of SRN must be equal to 7.
