Meaning

Indicates the medium attachment unit (MAU) of an Ethernet port. All MAUs are named according to IEEE standards. Value AUI indicates the attachment unit interface. Value Foirl indicates fiber optic inter repeater link. Value 10Broad36 indicates a 10 Mbit/s Ethernet that uses coaxial cables. All other MAUs are named in the format of "digits+Base+type", where, digits indicate the maximum transmit rate (for example, 10 indicates the maximum transmit rate of 10 Mbit/s), Base indicates that baseband transmission is used to transmit signals, and types have the following meanings: T indicates unshielded twisted pair (UTP) cables, 5 indicates thick coaxial cables, 2 indicates thin coaxial cables, X indicates the 8B/10B encoding scheme with 8-bit data and 2-bit error correction data, TX indicates category 5 twisted pair, FD indicates full duplex (for example, 1000BaseXFD indicates full duplex of 1000BaseX), and HD indicates half duplex. For details about other types, see RFC3636.
