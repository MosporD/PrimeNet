LST IPRSCGRPALG
Meaning

Indicates the congestion clear time threshold of an IP transmission resource group. When TCSW is set to ENABLE, if the buffer time of non-real-time service data in an IP transmission resource group is less than the threshold, the IP transmission resource group is not congested, and congestion clear signals are transmitted.

Impact on Radio Network Performance

A smaller parameter value indicates a better differentiation effect for bandwidth allocation of non-real-time services. This, however, leads to a higher probability of emptying the buffer of the transmission resource group and affects the bandwidth usage.

A larger parameter value indicates a worse differentiation effect for bandwidth allocation of non-real-time services. This, however, leads to a lower probability of emptying the buffer of the transmission resource group and is unlikely to affect the bandwidth usage.
