Parameter Relationship

-Configuration constraints:

  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]For an RRUCHAIN MO with TT set to TRUNK_CHAIN, the configurations of the RRUCHAIN MO must meet the following requirements:
  • 1. When CONNPORTNUM is set to 3, the values in combination 1 (CONNCN1, CONNSRN1, and CONNSN1), combination 2 (CONNCN2, CONNSRN2, and CONNSN2), and combination 3 (CONNCN3, CONNSRN3, and CONNSN3) must be equal to their counterparts. In addition, the values of CONNPN1 and CONNSPN1, CONNPN2 and CONNSPN2, and CONNPN3 and CONNSPN3 must not be equal to their counterparts.
  • 2. When CONNPORTNUM is set to 4, the values in combination 1 (CONNCN1, CONNSRN1, and CONNSN1), combination 2 (CONNCN2, CONNSRN2, and CONNSN2), combination 3 (CONNCN3, CONNSRN3, and CONNSN3), and combination 4 (CONNCN4, CONNSRN4, and CONNSN4) must be equal to their counterparts. In addition, the values of CONNPN1 and CONNSPN1, CONNPN2 and CONNSPN2, CONNPN3 and CONNSPN3, and CONNPN4 and CONNSPN4 must not be equal to their counterparts.
  • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]An RRUCHAIN MO with TT set to TRUNK_CHAIN must meet the following conditions:
  • 1. When the value of CONNPORTNUM in the RRUCHAIN MO is greater than or equal to 1, the CPRIPORT MO associated with CONNCN1, CONNSRN1, CONNSN1, CONNPN1, and CONNSPN1 in the RRUCHAIN MO must not be occupied.
  • 2. When the value of CONNPORTNUM in the RRUCHAIN MO is greater than or equal to 2, the CPRIPORT MO associated with CONNCN2, CONNSRN2, CONNSN2, CONNPN2, and CONNSPN2 in the RRUCHAIN MO must not be occupied.
  • 3. When the value of CONNPORTNUM in the RRUCHAIN MO is greater than or equal to 3, the CPRIPORT MO associated with CONNCN3, CONNSRN3, CONNSN3, CONNPN3, and CONNSPN3 in the RRUCHAIN MO must not be occupied.
  • 4. When the value of CONNPORTNUM in the RRUCHAIN MO is 4, the CPRIPORT MO associated with CONNCN4, CONNSRN4, CONNSN4, CONNPN4, and CONNSPN4 in the RRUCHAIN MO must not be occupied.

-Parameter relationship with conditions:

  • If CONNPORTNUM is set to 2, 3, 4, CONNSN2 must be set.
