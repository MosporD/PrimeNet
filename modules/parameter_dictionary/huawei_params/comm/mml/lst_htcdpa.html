List TCU Heat Control Delta Parameter (LST HTCDPA)

Network Element: BTS3900, BTS3900 WCDMA, BTS3900 LTE, BTS3900 5G, BTS5900, BTS5900 WCDMA, BTS5900 LTE, BTS5900 5G

Function

Use this command to list the configuration of the temperature control unit (TCU) heat control delta parameters. This command is applicable only to a TCU of the direct-ventilation type or a TCU with a breathable film.

Note

None.

To list the heat control delta parameters of the TCU in cabinet 0, run the following command:
LST HTCDPA: CN=0;

Output Description

Field Description

Cabinet No.

Indicates the number of the cabinet where the TCU is located.

Subrack No.

Indicates the number of the subrack where the TCU is located.

Slot No.

Indicates the number of the slot where the TCU is located.

Low Temperature Critical Point[degree Celsius(degree Fahrenheit)]

Indicates the threshold of the low temperature state.

High Temperature Critical Point[degree Celsius(degree Fahrenheit)]

Indicates the threshold of the high temperature state.

Temperature Limit at Air Outlet[degree Celsius(degree Fahrenheit)]

Indicates the expected lowest temperature at the air outlet.

Dead Band[degree Celsius(degree Fahrenheit)]

Indicates the tolerable error value of temperature control.

Normal Temperature Delta Tin[degree Celsius(degree Fahrenheit)]

Indicates the difference between the temperature at the air inlet and that of the environment at the normal temperature.

Normal Temperature Delta Tout[degree Celsius(degree Fahrenheit)]

Indicates the difference between the temperature at the air outlet and that of the air inlet at the normal temperature.

High Temperature Delta Tout[degree Celsius(degree Fahrenheit)]

Indicates the difference between the temperature at the air outlet and that of the environment at the high temperature.
