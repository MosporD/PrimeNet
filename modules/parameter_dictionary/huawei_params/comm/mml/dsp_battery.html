Display Battery (DSP BATTERY)

Network Element: BTS3900, BTS3900 WCDMA, BTS3900 LTE, BTS3900 5G, BTS5900, BTS5900 WCDMA, BTS5900 LTE, BTS5900 5G

Function

Use this command to query the dynamic information about a battery.

Note

  • Before querying the dynamic information about a battery, ensure that the battery has been added.

  • If the operational status of the PMU that manages the battery is unavailable, the query fails.

Output Description

Field Description

Cabinet No.

Indicates the number of the cabinet where the PMU managing the battery is located.

Subrack No.

Indicates the number of the subrack where the PMU managing the battery is located.

Slot No.

Indicates the number of the slot where the PMU managing the battery is located.

Battery Type

Indicates the type of the battery.

Number Of Battery Groups

Indicates the number of battery groups.

Remaining Capacity(%)

Indicates the remaining capacity of the battery by displaying the percentage of the current capacity to the total capacity. When the battery is powered off, this parameter indicates the latest remaining capacity before it is powered off.

Remaining Time(min)

Indicates the remaining discharging time of a battery, essentially, the duration in which the battery can provide power supply when the mains supply is abnormal.

Battery Temperature[degree Celsius(degree Fahrenheit)]

Indicates the current temperature of the battery.

Battery Current(A)

Indicates the charging or discharging current of batteries.

Charging/Discharging State

Indicates the charging or discharging management state of batteries, which is determined by the power supply system. The charging or discharging management state can be equalized charging, float charging, force equalized charging, sleeping, or discharging. Equalized charging quickly recovers the battery capacity. During equalized charging, the charging voltage is relatively high. Generally, the power supply system automatically enables a battery to start equalized charging after the battery is discharged. Float charging compensates for the battery power loss due to self-discharge and keeps the battery capacity. It can also recover the battery capacity with a relatively low charging voltage. (The power supply system automatically enables a battery to start float charging when the power supply is abnormal, for example, in the following cases: mains phase loss, mains undervoltage/overvoltage, power module faults, broken battery loop, and charging overcurrent.) Force equalized charging is an enhanced mode of equalized charging. The charging current and voltage during force equalized charging are higher than those during equalized charging while the charging time is shorter than that of equalized charging. It is applicable when the electricity network is unsatisfactory and the battery is discharged frequently. A battery in the sleeping state is neither being charged nor discharged. A battery enters the discharging state when the DC output voltage of the power supply system is lower than the battery voltage.

Battery Power State

Indicates the power state of the battery. The battery can be powered off manually, or automatically for protection purpose based on the environment. A battery powered off cannot provide power for the load.

Battery Test Phase

Indicates the phase of the battery test.

Battery Accumulated Charge Time(h)

Indicates the accumulated charging time of a battery, including floating charging time and equalized charging time.

Battery Accumulated Sleeping Time(h)

Indicates the accumulated sleeping time of a battery. Battery sleeping effectively prolongs the battery lifespan.

Number Of Lithium Batteries

Indicates the number of lithium batteries in a PMU.

Lithium Battery Rated Capacity

Indicates the capacity that all lithium batteries in a PMU can discharge under a specified condition, such as the specified temperature, discharge rate, or end voltage.

Lithium Battery Efficiency

Indicates the ratios of charge capacity to rated capacity of lithium batteries that are fully charged.

Power Cut Times

Indicates the number of times that the Mains Input is powered off.

Charge Times In Half an Hour

Indicates the number of times that the battery is charged in half an hour.

Charge Times In 1 Hour

Indicates the number of times that the battery is charged when the battery is charged for more than half an hour and less than one hour or for one hour.

Charge Times In 2 Hours
