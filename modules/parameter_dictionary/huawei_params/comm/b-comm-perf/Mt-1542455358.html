Description

These counters measure the maximum, minimum, and average transmit and receive rates in an MP group within a measurement period. They indicate the traffic volume of MP links.

Measurement Points

The number of bytes transmitted and received by the MP group is sampled per second. At the end of a measurement period, the maximum, minimum, and average sampling results are reported.
