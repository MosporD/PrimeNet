Description

These counters measure the maximum and average transmit rate and receive rate on the trunk within a measurement period. They indicate the link state of the trunk.

Measurement Points

The transmit rate or the receive rate on the trunk is sampled every second. At the end of a measurement period, the maximum or average of these sampling results is used as the value of the corresponding counter.

Formula

None

Unit

kbit/s
