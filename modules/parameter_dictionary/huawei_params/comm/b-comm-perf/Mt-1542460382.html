Description

These counters provide the maximum and average transmit/receive bandwidths on an SRIO port in a measurement period. The counters only indicate the bandwidth allocation instead of real-time usage.

Measurement Points

The allocated bandwidth is sampled every 10 seconds. At the end of a measurement period, the maximum and average of these sampling results are used as the values of the corresponding counters.
