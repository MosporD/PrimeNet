Description

These counters measure the maximum, minimum, and average transmit and receive rates on a PPP link within a measurement period. They indicate the traffic volume of the PPP link.

Measurement Points

Number of bytes transmitted and received on the PPP link per second. At the end of a measurement period, the maximum, minimum, and average sampling results are reported.

Formula

None

Unit

bit/s
