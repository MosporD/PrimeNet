Description

This counter measures the duration when an SCTP link is unavailable within a measurement period. A link is unavailable when it is faulty or not activated.

Measurement Points

The duration through which an SCTP link is unavailable is sampled per second. At the end of a measurement period, the sum of these sampling results is used as the value of the corresponding counter. Due to time adjustment and measurement precision, the measurement result of the counter VS.SctpLnk.Unavail.Dur may have an error of several seconds.
