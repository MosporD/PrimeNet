Description

The preceding counter measures the power consumption of objects such as RRU, RFU, AAU, CXU, or RHUB within a measurement period.

Measurement Points

The system samples the power consumption of board every 5 minutes. At the end of a measurement period, the sum of these calculated results is used as the value of the counter. The sampling period is not synchronous with the reporting period, and therefore a deviation of one sampling period may exist.

Note: To compare the power consumption, you are advised to observe the value of this counter for a long period of time. The value of this counter may change slightly in a short period of time, and it is difficult to observe the change.
