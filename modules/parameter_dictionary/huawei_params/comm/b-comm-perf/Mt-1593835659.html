Description

This counter provides uplink UASs over the CPRI0 port. If 10 consecutive uplink SESs occur over the CPRI0 port, uplink UASs start from the first SES. If 10 consecutive uplink non-SESs occur over the CPRI0 port, uplink UASs stop at the first non-SES. UASs do not mean that services are unavailable. This counter cannot be measured for the following modules: RRU3702, RRU3008, RRU3004, RRU3801C, RRU3801E, RRU3804, RRU3805, RRU3821E, RRU3806, RRU3808, RRU3203, RRU3201, RRU3222, RRU3220, RRU3908, RRU3201, RRU3920, RRU3926, RRU3606, DRFU, GRFU V1, GRFU V2, GRFU V2A, GRFU V2B, GRFU V0, GRFU V2e, WRFU, LRFU, MRFU V2A, MRFU V0, MRFU V1, MRFU V2, MRFU V2e, CRFU, RHUB and pRRU.

Measurement Points

Uplink bit errors over the CPRI0 port are sampled every 1s. If a UAS occurs, the value of this counter is incremented by 1.
