PMU

EQUIPMENT
  +-CABINET
    +-SUBRACK
      +-PMU
        +-ALMPORT[0~5]
        +-BATTERY[0~1]
        +-DIESELGEN[0~1]

Description

The PMU MO monitors and manages the voltage, current, load, power source temperature, battery, and diesel generator of the power system.

Effective Scenario

Immediately

Relations Between MOs

  • Base class:

    RU

  • Possible parents:

    SUBRACK

  • Possible children:

    ALMPORT[0~5];BATTERY[0~1];DIESELGEN[0~1]
  • Relation with the subrack that houses the PMU MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]A subrack must be configured together with a PMU MO to house the PMU MO.
  • Relation with the SUBRACK MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The number of PMU MOs configured on one SUBRACK MO must be greater than or equal to 0 and smaller than or equal to 1.
  • Relation with the ALMPORT MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The number of ALMPORT MOs configured on one PMU MO must be greater than or equal to 0 and smaller than or equal to 5.
  • Relation with the BATTERY MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]When a PMU MO with PTYPE set to SC48200 is configured, a BATTERY MO must be configured.
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The upper-level PMU MO of the BATTERY MO must have been configured.
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The number of BATTERY MOs configured on one PMU MO must be greater than or equal to 0 and smaller than or equal to 1.
  • Relation with the PSU MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The PMU MO corresponding to a PSU MO must have been configured.
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The upper-level PMU MO of the PSU MO must have been configured.
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the PMU MO is set to SC48200, there must be no lower-level PSU MO for the PMU MO.
  • Relation with the BDU MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the PMU MO is set to SC48200, there must be no lower-level BDU MO for the PMU MO.
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The upper-level PMU MO of the BDU MO must have been configured.
  • Relation with the DIESELGEN MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]Only a PMU MO with PTYPE set to SC48200 can be configured with a lower-level DIESELGEN MO.
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The number of DIESELGEN MOs configured on one PMU MO must be greater than or equal to 0 and smaller than or equal to 1.
  • Relation with the TCU, PMU, EMU, FMU, CCU, MEB and GDU MOs:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]It is not allowed in the following cases: 1. The combination of MCN, MSRN, MPN, and ADDR in the PMU MO is same as the combination of those in other PMU MOs; 2. The combination of MCN, MSRN, MPN, and ADDR in the PMU MO is same as the combination of those in the EMU MOs; 3. The combination of MCN, MSRN, MPN, and ADDR in the PMU MO is same as the combination of those in the TCU MOs; 4. The combination of MCN, MSRN, MPN, and ADDR in the PMU MO is same as the combination of those in the FMU MOs; 5. The combination of MCN, MSRN, and MPN in the PMU MO is same as the combination of those in the CCU MOs, and ADDR in the PMU MO is equal to 21; 6. The combination of MCN, MSRN, and MPN in the PMU MO is same as the combination of those in the MEB MOs, and ADDR in the PMU MO is equal to 10; 7. The combination of MCN, MSRN, MPN, and ADDR in the PMU MO is same as the combination of those in the GDU MOs.
  • Relation with the RRU MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The RRU MO specified by MCN and MSRN in the PMU MO cannot be configured as the MRIU.
  • Relation with the CABINET MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]When TYPE in the CABINET MO housing the PMU MO is set to OPM, SRN in the PMU MO must be set to 7.
  • Relation with the SUBRACK, RRU, CCU, and RHUB MOs:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB]The MO corresponding to MCN and MSRN in the PMU MO must have been configured and be one of the following types: 1. SUBRACK MO with SRN set to 0 or 1; 2. RRU MO with SN set to 0; 3. CCU MO; 4. RHUB MO.
  • Relation with the SUBRACK, RRU, and CCU MOs:
    • [gNodeB]The MO corresponding to MCN and MSRN in the PMU MO must have been configured and be one of the following types: 1. SUBRACK MO with SRN set to 0 or 1; 2. RRU MO with SN set to 0; 3. CCU MO.

Related MML Commands

Table 1 describes the MML commands related to this MO.

Table 1 Related MML commands

MML Command

Function
ADD PMU

Use this command to add a power monitoring unit (PMU). When the PMU is added, a PMU subrack and a PMU board are added at the same time.
CLR HVDCOUTPUTPROTECT

Use this command to close the circuit breaker in a 225-400 V DC power distribution unit (HVDU). If no alarm related to the safety of 225-400 V DC power distribution is reported, the 225-400 V DC power output to the load is resumed. If an alarm related to the safety of 225-400 V DC power distribution is reported, the circuit breaker is still open and the 225-400 V DC power distribution is under protection.
CLR PMUALM

Use this command to clear a power monitoring unit (PMU) smoke alarm. When a PMU smoke alarm is reported, you must clear the fault that triggers the alarm, and then run this command to clear the alarm.
DSP HVDCSTATE

Use this command to query the operating status of a 225-400 V DC power distribution unit
DSP PMU

Use this command to query the dynamic information about a power monitoring unit (PMU), which include the information about the PMU, PSU, and batteries.
LST PMU

Use this command to list the configuration of a power monitoring unit (PMU).
MOD PMU

Use this command to modify the configuration of a power monitoring unit (PMU).
RMV PMU

Use this command to remove a power monitoring unit (PMU). When the PMU is removed, a PMU subrack and a PMU board are removed at the same time.
RST PMUPWR

Use this command to shut down the DC output of the power system and then resume the DC output after a specified period of time.

The communication address is used by the PMU to communicate with the main control and transmission board. The value of this parameter must be consistent with the actual settings of the DIP switch. The 4-bit DIP switch can be treated as a 4-bit binary number. The settings of ON and OFF are represented by 1 and 0 respectively. Convert the 4-bit number from binary to decimal, and then the decimal number is used as the value of this parameter.
Power System Type

Indicates the type of the power system for the PMU.
AC Voltage Alarm Lower Threshold

Indicates the lower threshold of AC input voltage. When the AC input voltage is lower than the value, an ALM-25622 Mains Input Out of Range alarm is reported.
AC Voltage Alarm Upper Threshold

Indicates the higher threshold of AC input voltage. When the AC input voltage is higher than the value, an ALM-25622 Mains Input Out of Range alarm is reported.
DC Voltage Alarm Lower Threshold

Indicates the lower threshold of the DC voltage. When the output voltage of the busbar is lower than the value, an ALM-25621 Power Supply DC Output Out of Range alarm is reported.
DC Voltage Alarm Upper Threshold

Indicates the upper threshold of the DC voltage. When the output voltage of the busbar is higher than the value, an ALM-25621 Power Supply DC Output Out of Range alarm is reported.
Load Shutdown Flag

Indicates the flag for power-off against low voltage, which determines whether the load is powered off in the case of low voltage.
Load Shutdown Voltage

Indicates the load voltage below which the load is powered off automatically when load power-off against low voltage is enabled.
Cabinet Temperature Alarm Lower Threshold

Indicates the lower threshold of the cabinet temperature. If the cabinet temperature is lower than the value, an ALM-25652 Cabinet Temperature Unacceptable alarm is reported. This parameter is valid only for the APM100 and APM200 cabinets.
Cabinet Temperature Alarm Upper Threshold

Indicates the upper threshold of the cabinet temperature. If the cabinet temperature is higher than the value, an ALM-25652 Cabinet Temperature Unacceptable alarm is reported. This parameter is valid only for the APM100 and APM200 cabinets.
Cabinet Humidity Alarm Lower Threshold

Indicates the lower threshold of the cabinet humidity. If the cabinet humidity is lower than the value, an ALM-25653 Cabinet Humidity Unacceptable alarm is reported.
Cabinet Humidity Alarm Upper Threshold

Indicates the upper threshold of the cabinet humidity. If the cabinet humidity is higher than the value, an ALM-25653 Cabinet Humidity Unacceptable alarm is reported.
Special Analog Alarm Flag

Indicates whether to allow the report of a dedicated analog alarm. If the shield flag for an analog alarm is enabled, the analog alarm cannot be reported.
Special Boolean Alarm Flag

Indicates whether to allow the reporting of a dedicated Boolean alarm. If the shield flag for a Boolean alarm is enabled, the Boolean alarm cannot be reported.
Load Shutdown Time Flag

This parameter determines whether the load is powered off based on time in a base station. When this parameter is set to DISABLE, the load is powered off only when the battery voltage is less than Load Shutdown Voltage. When this parameter is set to ENABLE, the load is powered off when the duration of AC power shutdown exceeds Load Shutdown Time or the battery voltage is less than Load Shutdown Voltage.
Load Shutdown Time

Indicates the duration after which the load is powered off. When both the flag for power-off against time and the flag for power-off against low voltage are enabled, the load is powered off when the duration of AC power shutdown exceeds Load Shutdown Time or the battery voltage is less than Load Shutdown Voltage.
Power Distribution Slot No-Backup Flag

Indicates whether to enable the no-backup capability of the power distribution slots for a power system.If this parameter is set to DISABLE, the power system retains the backup power capability of the storage batteries in the power distribution slots when an AC power failure occurs. If this parameter is set to ENABLE, the power system powers off the load that is powered by the power distribution slots when an AC power failure occurs.
HVDU Number

Indicates the number of 225-400 V DC power distribution units (HVDUs). Set this parameter based on site conditions. An incorrect configuration will affect the reporting of the alarm related to the safety of 225-400 V DC power distribution..
HVDC Insulation Resistance Alarm Threshold

Indicates the insulation resistance alarm threshold. The insulation resistance is that between the positive/negative bus bar and the ground, which retains the default value. If the load resistance value is less than the threshold, contact Huawei technical support to check the threshold.
HVDC Output Voltage

Indicates the 225-400 V direct current (DC) output voltage. Set this parameter based on site conditions. An incorrect parameter value may lead to overvoltage protection or even damage. If this parameter is set to a value greater than 300 V, disable the DC output of the DC power supply unit (HSU) and then enable the DC output to make the parameter setting take effect.
AC Current Limiting Flag

Indicates whether the AC power system limits the AC input current. When this switch is turned on, the AC power system limits the AC input current to prevent the entire base station from being powered off due to the upper-level circuit breaker tripping. When this switch is turned off, the AC power system does not limit the AC input current. The maximum AC input current depends on the hardware capability of the AC power system. If the AC input current exceeds the rated current of the upper-level circuit breaker, the entire base station may be powered off.
AC Air Switch Current Limiting Threshold

Indicates the AC input current threshold of the AC power system. After the AC current limiting function takes effect, the AC power system limits the AC input current so that the AC input current does not exceed the AC current threshold.
AC Phase Type

Indicates the external AC phase type of the AC power system.

Charging/Discharging State

Indicates the charging or discharging management state of batteries, which is determined by the power supply system. The charging or discharging management state can be equalized charging, float charging, force equalized charging, sleeping, or discharging. Equalized charging quickly recovers the battery capacity. During equalized charging, the charging voltage is relatively high. Generally, the power supply system automatically enables a battery to start equalized charging after the battery is discharged. Float charging compensates for the battery power loss due to self-discharge and keeps the battery capacity. It can also recover the battery capacity with a relatively low charging voltage. (The power supply system automatically enables a battery to start float charging when the power supply is abnormal, for example, in the following cases: mains phase loss, mains undervoltage/overvoltage, power module faults, broken battery loop, and charging overcurrent.) Force equalized charging is an enhanced mode of equalized charging. The charging current and voltage during force equalized charging are higher than those during equalized charging while the charging time is shorter than that of equalized charging. It is applicable when the electricity network is unsatisfactory and the battery is discharged frequently. A battery in the sleeping state is neither being charged nor discharged. A battery enters the discharging state when the DC output voltage of the power supply system is lower than the battery voltage.
