PSU

EQUIPMENT
  +-CABINET
    +-SUBRACK
      +-PSU

Description

A PSU MO converts +220 V AC or +110 V AC power to -48 V DC power. The number of PSUs to be configured depends on the load capacity.

Effective Scenario

Immediately

Relations Between MOs

  • Base class:

    RU

  • Possible parents:

    SUBRACK
  • Relation with the PMU MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The upper-level PMU MO of the PSU MO must have been configured.
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]If PTYPE in the PMU MO is set to SC48200, there must be no lower-level PSU MO for the PMU MO.
  • Relation with the PSU MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]A unique combination is necessary for the following parameters: 1. CN, SRN, and SN in the PSU MO; 2. CN, SRN, and SN in the BDU MO.
  • Relation with the SUBRACK MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The number of PSU MOs configured on one SUBRACK MO must be greater than or equal to 0 and smaller than or equal to 10.

Electronic Label(from RU)

"IssueNumber" indicates the board hardware version. "CLEICode" indicates the common language equipment indicator (CLEI) code of the board. "BOM" indicates the fine-grained item code of the board. "Model" indicates the model displayed on the customer interface, such as Bill of Quotation (BOQ) and paper labels.
