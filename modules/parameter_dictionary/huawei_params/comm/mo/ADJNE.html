ADJNE

EQUIPMENT
  +-ADJNE

Description

An ADJNE MO consists of the information about an adjacent node. NEs are interconnected to construct a complicated network for telecommunications. Each node on the network stores the information about each of its adjacent node so that the topology of the entire network is available on the EMS.

Effective Scenario

Immediately

Relations Between MOs

  • Possible parents:

    EQUIPMENT
  • Relation with the ADJNE MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB]Under a base station, the total number of ADJNE MOs must be smaller than or equal to 1.
