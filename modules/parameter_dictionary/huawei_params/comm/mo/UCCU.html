UCCU

EQUIPMENT
  +-CABINET
    +-SUBRACK
      +-UCCU
        +-CASCADEPORT[6~6]
        +-SFP[6~6]

Description

The UCCU board converts baseband data from the SRIO/SCPRI format into the Ethernet format and interconnects remote ports and USU3910/switches.

Effective Scenario

Immediately

Relations Between MOs

  • Base class:

    RU

  • Possible parents:

    SUBRACK

  • Possible children:

    CASCADEPORT[6~6];SFP[6~6]
  • Relation with other boards in the BBU subrack:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB]A unique combination is necessary for the following parameters: 1. CN, SRN, and SN in the BBP MO; 2. CN, SRN, and SN in the TRP MO; 3. CN, SRN, and SN in the UCIU MO; 4. CN, SRN, and SN in the USCU MO; 5. CN, SRN, and SN in the MPT MO; 6. CN, SRN, and SN in the BRI MO; 7. CN, SRN, and SN in the GTMU MO; 8. CN, SRN, and SN in the UCCU MO; 9. CN, SRN, and SN in the RFC MO.
  • Relation with the CASCADEPORT MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB]When the combination of CN, SRN and SN in the CASCADEPORT MO is same as the combination of CN, SRN and SN in the UCCU MO, the PM of CASCADEPORT must be set to AUTO.
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eNodeB]When the combination of CN, SRN and SN in the CASCADEPORT MO is same as the combination of CN, SRN and SN in the UCCU MO, the PT of CASCADEPORT must be set to ExpandedCascadePort.
  • Relation with the SUBRACK MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB]The MPT, GTMU, BBP, UCIU, USCU, BRI, TRP, or UCCU MO cannot be configured under a SUBRACK MO with TYPE set to BLADEBBU.
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eNodeB]The GTMU, UCIU, UCCU or TRP cannot be configured under a SUBRACK MO with TYPE set to BBU5900.
    • [NodeB]The UCCU MO cannot be configured under a SUBRACK with TYPE set to BBU3900-1U.
  • Relation with the SUBRACK, GTMU, UCIU, USCU, BRI, and TRP MOs:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB]When TYPE in the SUBRACK MO is set to BBU5900A, the GTMU, UCIU, USCU, BRI, TRP, RFC, or UCCU MO cannot be configured.
