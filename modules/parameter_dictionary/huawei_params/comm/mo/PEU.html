PEU

EQUIPMENT
  +-CABINET
    +-SUBRACK
      +-PEU
        +-ALMPORT[0~8]

Description

The PEU MO is the power and environment unit (PEU) that is used to convert -48 V DC or +24 V DC power to +12 V DC power for the boards in the BBU subrack. A BBU subrack can be configured with a maximum of two PEUs. Each PEU supports eight dry contact alarms for environment monitoring. The bladebbu does not support this configuration.

Effective Scenario

Immediately

Relations Between MOs

  • Base class:

    RU

  • Possible parents:

    SUBRACK

  • Possible children:

    ALMPORT[0~8]
  • Relation with the SUBRACK MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The number of PEU MOs configured on one SUBRACK MO must be greater than or equal to 0 and smaller than or equal to 2.
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB]The PEU MO cannot be configured under a SUBRACK with TYPE set to BLADEBBU.
  • Relation with the ALMPORT MO:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]The number of ALMPORT MOs configured on one PEU MO must be 8.
  • Relation with other boards in the BBU subrack:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB, gNodeB]A unique combination is necessary for the following parameters: 1. CN, SRN, and SN in the PEU MO; 2. CN, SRN, and SN in the UEIU MO.

Indicates the input voltage detected on the UPEU. Only digital power supply boards support query of the input voltage. NULL indicates that the power supply board does not support the query.
Alarm Status(from RU)

Indicates the alarm status of the board hardware, which can be set to Normal, Warning, Minor, Major, or Critical. The alarm status of the PSU, PDB, and BDU is in the alarm status of the PMU.
Operational State(from RU)

Indicates the operational state of the board, which can be set to Enabled or Disabled. Enabled indicates that services can be carried on the board. Disabled indicates that no service can be carried on the board.
Availability Status(from RU)

Indicates the physical status of a board, which can be Uninstalled, Starting up, Normal, Inconsistent, Unconfigured, Power off, Fault, Communication lost, In test, or the combination of multiple states. For example, if a board is not configured and fails to communicate with the main control and transmission board, the availability status of the board is "Unconfigured, Communication lost." If a board is under management of the peer end, the availability status of the board may be Starting up, Communication lost, or Fault.
Electronic Label(from RU)

"IssueNumber" indicates the board hardware version. "CLEICode" indicates the common language equipment indicator (CLEI) code of the board. "BOM" indicates the fine-grained item code of the board. "Model" indicates the model displayed on the customer interface, such as Bill of Quotation (BOQ) and paper labels.
