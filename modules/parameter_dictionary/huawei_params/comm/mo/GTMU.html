GTMU

EQUIPMENT
  +-CABINET
    +-SUBRACK
      +-GTMU
        +-CPRIPORT[6~6]
        +-SFP[6~6]
        +-USB[1~1]

Description

The GTMU MO performs main controlling, switching, and transmission, and provides clock sources and ports for receiving and sending GSM RF signals. This MO applies only to GSM.

Effective Scenario

Immediately
