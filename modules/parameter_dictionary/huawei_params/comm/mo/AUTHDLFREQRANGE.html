AUTHDLFREQRANGE

EQUIPMENT
  +-AUTHDLFREQRANGE

Description

The downlink frequency band that an operator has been authorized to use. It is used in RF-related maintenance commands so that frequencies of other operators will not be used during the execution of these commands, thereby avoiding interrupting services of other operators.

Effective Scenario

Immediately
