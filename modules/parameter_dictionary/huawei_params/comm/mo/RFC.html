RFC

EQUIPMENT
  +-CABINET
    +-SUBRACK
      +-RFC
        +-RFCPORT[0~8]

Description

The <b>RFC</b> MO is used to configure a radio frequency (RF) card, which can transmit RF signals to and receive RF signals from a third-party base station. An RF card transfers signals between the third-party base station and RRUs, and helps provide indoor coverage.

Effective Scenario

Immediately

Relations Between MOs

  • Base class:

    RU

  • Possible parents:

    SUBRACK

  • Possible children:

    RFCPORT[0~8]
  • Relation with other boards in the BBU subrack:
    • [Co-MPT, NodeB, eGBTS, eNodeB]A unique combination is necessary for the following parameters: 1. CN, SRN, and SN in the BBP MO; 2. CN, SRN, and SN in the TRP MO; 3. CN, SRN, and SN in the UCIU MO; 4. CN, SRN, and SN in the USCU MO; 5. CN, SRN, and SN in the BRI MO; 6. CN, SRN, and SN in the UCCU MO; 7. CN, SRN, and SN in the RFC MO.

Availability Status(from RU)

Indicates the physical status of a board, which can be Uninstalled, Starting up, Normal, Inconsistent, Unconfigured, Power off, Fault, Communication lost, In test, or the combination of multiple states. For example, if a board is not configured and fails to communicate with the main control and transmission board, the availability status of the board is "Unconfigured, Communication lost." Electronic Label(from RU)
