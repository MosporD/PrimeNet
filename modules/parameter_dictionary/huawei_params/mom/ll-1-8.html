Effective Scenarios

The effective scenario indicates the scenario in which an MO takes effect. The effective scenario can be after the reset or immediately.

  • After the reset: indicates that the base station needs to be reset after the addition or removal of an MO. Otherwise, the addition or removal of the MO does not take effect.
  • Immediately: indicates that the addition or removal of an MO takes effect immediately. A reset in the system, such as a cell reset or board reset, is not required.
