CellAlgoExtSwitch

+-NE
  +-eNodeBFunction
    +-Cell
      +-CellAlgoExtSwitch

Description

The CellAlgoExtSwitch MO includes the parameters used to configure the extension switches related to cell-level algorithms.

Effective Scenario

Immediately

Relations Between MOs

  • Possible parents:

    Cell
  • Relation with the CellCsiRsParaCfg, CellAlgoSwitch, SectorSplitCell, and SectorSplitGroup MOs:
    • When CsiRsSwitch in a CellCsiRsParaCfg MO is set to FIXED_CFG or ADAPTIVE_CFG, TM9Switch or TM10Switch of EnhMIMOSwitch, FDDHomNetDlCompSwitch or FDDHetNetDlCompSwitch or Tm9JtSwitch of DlCompSwitch, SfnJtSwitch of SfnAlgoSwitch, or 4TxTM9MuMimoSwitch of EmimoSwitch in the associated CellAlgoSwitch MO must be selected, or Tm9FourLayerPairSwitch of WttxMuMimoSwitch in a CellAlgoExtSwitch MO must be selected, or JOINT_SCHEDULING_SWITCH of IntelligentBeamSchSwitch in SectorSplitGroup MO must be selected.

    • When 4TxTM9MuMimoSwitch of EmimoSwitch in a CellAlgoSwitch MO or Tm9FourLayerPairSwitch of WttxMuMimoSwitch in a CellAlgoExtSwitch MO is selected, FddTddInd in the associated Cell MO must be set to CELL_FDD.
