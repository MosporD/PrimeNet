CellLteFlexBwItfCfg

+-NE
  +-eNodeBFunction
    +-Cell
      +-CellLteFlexBwItfCfg

Description

The CellLteFlexBwItfCfg MO consists of parameters related to local LTE cell ID, ARFCN of a high-interference GSM cell adjacent to the LTE cell (ARFCN of the bandwidth used by GERAN under standardized bandwidth), and GSM frequency when non-standard LTE bandwidth is used. Such information must be configured by network planning engineers based on site requirements.

Effective Scenario

Immediately

Relations Between MOs

  • Possible parents:

    Cell
  • Relation with the Cell MO:
    • A maximum of six CellLteFlexBwItfCfg MO instances can be configured for a cell.
  • Relation with the Cell and CellLteFlexBw MOs:
    • When the LTE_FB_CFG_SWITCH option of the LteFlexBwSwitch parameter in a CellLteFlexBw MO is selected, the downlink GSM frequencies indicated by the GsmCarrierFreq and HighItfGsmArfcn parameters in a CellLteFlexBwItfCfg MO must meet the frequency range constraints of the following parameters: DlEarfcn, FreqBand, and DlBandWidth in the associated Cell MO and DlCustStartPrbIndex and DlCustEndPrbIndex in the CellLteFlexBw MO.
    • When the LTE_FB_CFG_SWITCH option of the LteFlexBwSwitch parameter in a CellLteFlexBw MO is selected, the uplink GSM frequencies indicated by the GsmCarrierFreq and HighItfGsmArfcn parameters in a CellLteFlexBwItfCfg MO must meet the frequency range constraints of the following parameters: UlEarfcn, FreqBand, and UlBandWidth in the associated Cell MO and UlCustStartPrbIndex and UlCustEndPrbIndex in the CellLteFlexBw MO.

Related Parameters

Table 2 describes the parameters related to this MO.

Table 2 Configuration parameters

Parameter

Description
Local Cell ID

Indicates the local cell ID. This parameter applies only to LTE FDD.
High Interference GSM ARFCN

Indicates the ARFCN of a high-interference GSM cell adjacent to the LTE cell. This parameter applies only to LTE FDD.
GSM Carrier Frequency

Indicates the carrier bearing GSM signals of a cell adjacent to the LTE cell. This parameter applies only to LTE FDD.
