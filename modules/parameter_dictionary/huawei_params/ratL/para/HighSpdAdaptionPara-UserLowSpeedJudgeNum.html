Meaning

Indicates the threshold of the number of consecutive low-speed judgments required for an initially admitted UE or a high-speed UE to be identified as a low-speed UE in a high-speed or ultra-high-speed cell. This parameter applies only to LTE FDD and LTE TDD.

Impact on Radio Network Performance

A smaller parameter value results in a higher probability that a UE is identified as a low-speed UE, a higher probability that a low-speed UE is handed over to another cell when the Handover Enhancement at Speed Mobility feature is enabled, but a higher probability that a high-speed UE is erroneously identified as a low-speed UE and handed over to another cell. A larger parameter value results in the opposite effects.
