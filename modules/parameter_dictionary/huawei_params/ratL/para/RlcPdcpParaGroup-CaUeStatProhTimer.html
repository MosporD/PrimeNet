Meaning

Indicates the length of the status reporting prohibition timer that applies to non-GBR bearers in RLC AM for CA UEs in the 2CC CA, 3CC CA, and relaxed-backhaul-based CA states in their PCell when the number of UEs in RRC_CONNECTED mode does not exceed the value of CaUeRlcParaAdptiveThd. This option applies only to LTE FDD and LTE TDD.

Impact on Radio Network Performance

The timer affects the transmission of status reports related to non-GBR bearers in RLC AM for CA UEs in the 2CC CA, 3CC CA, and relaxed-backhaul-based CA states. If the timer is set to a larger value, the reports will be sent less frequently. However, there is a higher probability that the send window size becomes 0, reducing the transmission rate and UE throughput. If the timer is set to a smaller value, the reports will be sent more frequently. There is a higher probability that data at the transmitter is promptly acknowledged, maintaining the transmission rate. However, there is also a higher probability of RB and CCE resource wastes.
