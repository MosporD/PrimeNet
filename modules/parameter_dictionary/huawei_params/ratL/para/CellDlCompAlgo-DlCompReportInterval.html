Meaning

Indicates the interval at which TM9- or TM10-capable UEs periodically send A3 or A6 measurement reports when at least one of DL CoMP with TM 10 and joint transmission with TM9 takes effect. For details about the reporting interval, see 3GPP TS 36.331. If this parameter is set to OFF, the A3 or A6 reporting interval is the same as the reporting interval (specified by the ReportInterval parameter in the CellMcPara MO) of common event A3 or A6. This parameter applies only to LTE FDD.

Impact on Radio Network Performance

A larger value of this parameter results in less frequent DL CoMP UE measurement reporting and a lower capability of tracking the changes in the geographic positions of DL CoMP UEs, decreasing the average throughput of cell edge UEs. A smaller value of this parameter results in more frequent DL CoMP UE measurement reporting and a higher capability of tracking the changes in the geographic positions of DL CoMP UEs, increasing the average throughput of cell edge UEs but increasing the UE power consumption.
