Impact on Radio Network Performance

If this parameter is set to a smaller value, timing advance (TA) commands are sent at a higher frequency, more radio resources are consumed, more power is consumed for UEs in discontinuous reception (DRX) state even when the DRX period is long. However, UEs can move at a higher speed, and the uplink timing performance of UEs in high-speed mobility can be ensured.

If this parameter is set to a larger value, TA commands are sent at a lower frequency, fewer radio resources are consumed, less power is consumed for UEs in DRX state. However, only UEs that move at a lower speed can be supported, and the uplink timing performance of UEs in high-speed mobility cannot be ensured. In addition, untimely adjustments of uplink offsets result in RRC reestablishments or service drops.
