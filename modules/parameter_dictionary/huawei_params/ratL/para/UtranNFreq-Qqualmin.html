Meaning

Indicates the minimum quality level required for a cell on the UTRAN frequency to become a candidate for reselection. A neighboring cell on the UTRAN frequency can become a candidate for reselection only when the signal quality of the cell is better than the value of this parameter. For details, see 3GPP TS 25.304. This parameter applies only to LTE FDD.
