Caution

Modifying the parameters in the ENodeBFrameOffset MO for an LTE TDD cell will cause all activated LTE TDD cells served by the eNodeB to reset. If the eNodeB is equipped with a centralized scheduler and TDD CSPC or DL CoMP is enabled, the modification will also cause TDD CSPC or DL CoMP services to restart.
Validation of Modification

The parameter modification takes effect after the cell is automatically reset.
Impact on Radio Network Performance

If LTE TDD coexists with another TDD system (for example, WiMAX, TD-SCDMA, or PHS) and their spectrums are close to each other, inconsistency in uplink and downlink timeslots between systems may lead to high interference between them. This parameter is used to adjust the start time of all LTE TDD cell frames so that the uplink and downlink timeslots of LTE TDD are aligned with those of the other TDD system.

Carrier aggregation (CA) in LTE TDD requires time synchronization between carriers. Therefore, all cells must be configured with the same frame offset to implement CA.

Inconsistent frame offsets between carriers in FDD+TDD CA scenarios may decrease the proportion of UEs on which FDD+TDD CA takes effect.
