Meaning

Indicates the factor for the range of dedicated NPRACH resources used by UEs with the multi-tone transmission capability and also indicates the initial number of tones used for the scheduling of Msg3. For details about this parameter, see 3GPP TS 36.331. This parameter applies only to NB-IoT.

Impact on Radio Network Performance

A smaller factor for the range of dedicated NPRACH resources leads to a larger range of dedicated NPRACH resources for UEs with the multi-tone transmission capability. A larger factor for the range of dedicated NPRACH resources leads to a smaller range of dedicated NPRACH resources for UEs with the multi-tone transmission capability.

A smaller initial number of tones for the scheduling of Msg3 leads to a longer delay of Msg3 transmission. A larger initial number of tones for the scheduling of Msg3 leads to a shorter delay of Msg3 transmission.
