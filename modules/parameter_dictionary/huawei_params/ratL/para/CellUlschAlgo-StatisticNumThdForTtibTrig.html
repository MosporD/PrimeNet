Meaning

Indicates the threshold expressed in the number of times the channel quality of a UE consistently meets conditions for entering TTI bundling. Before a UE enters TTI bundling, the eNodeB takes statistics on the channel quality of the UE at intervals of 20 ms or longer. If the number of consecutive times the channel quality of the UE consistently meets the TTI bundling entry conditions is greater than the value of this parameter, the UE enters TTI bundling. This parameter postpones the entry into TTI bundling and reduces the probability of unnecessary TTI bundling entering caused by radio signal fluctuations. This parameter applies only to LTE FDD.

Impact on Radio Network Performance

This parameter reduces the probability of unnecessary TTI bundling entering caused by radio signal fluctuations, preventing unnecessary RRC connection reconfigurations. A smaller value of this parameter results in a shorter time of postponing TTI bundling entering and therefore a higher probability of entering TTI bundling (including unnecessary entry). This consumes more RB resources and decreases throughput if UEs enter TTI bundling when their channel quality is favorable. A larger value of this parameter results in a longer time of postponing TTI bundling entering and therefore a lower probability of entering TTI bundling and a higher probability that voice quality or cell-edge video user experience deteriorates. This consumes more CCEs and decrease voice MOSs or cell-edge video throughput if UEs do not enter TTI bundling even when channel quality is poor.
