Meaning

Indicates whether to enable the functions related to service differentiation.

VideoRateAdaptSwitch: If this option is deselected, video service rate adaption is disabled. If this option is selected, video service rate adaption is enabled. This option applies only to LTE FDD and LTE TDD.

DownLoadRateLimitSwitch: If this option is deselected, busy-hour download rate control is disabled. If this option is selected, busy-hour download rate control is enabled. This option applies only to LTE FDD and LTE TDD.

VoiceDataDifferentiationSwitch: Indicates whether to enable functions related to voice and data service differentiation. The functions are enabled only when this option is selected. The functions include TDD and FDD functions. TDD functions include those for CQI algorithms, PDCCH adjustment, exclusion of downlink QCI-5 services from the service scope of AMBR-based rate control, disabling the function controlled by DLDeltaCqiOptSwitch for SCCs of downlink CA UEs, CQI mask for QCI 1, and SCell deactivation based on the packet loss rate. FDD functions include exclusion of downlink QCI-5 services from the service scope of AMBR-based rate control, and disabling the function controlled by DLDeltaCqiOptSwitch for SCCs of downlink CA UEs, CQI mask for QCI 1, and SCell deactivation based on the packet loss rate. This option applies only to LTE FDD and LTE TDD.

AsForTcpSw: Indicates whether to enable TCP link setup acceleration guarantee. If this option is selected, TCP link setup acceleration is enabled. If this option is deselected, TCP link setup acceleration is disabled. This option applies only to LTE FDD and LTE TDD.

LowDelayServiceOptSwitch: Indicates whether to enable delay optimization for services that require low service delay. Delay optimization is enabled for these services only if this option is selected. This option applies only to LTE FDD and LTE TDD.

Impact on Radio Network Performance

VideoRateAdaptSwitch: When this option is selected, the eNodeB provides a higher-priority guarantee for video services and the scheduling probability increases. The impact varies as follows:

When the channel quality is favorable and video service rates are lower than the guaranteed bit rate, the increased scheduling probability improves the cell throughput.

When the channel quality is unfavorable and video service rates are lower than the service guaranteed bit rate, the increased scheduling probability reduces the cell throughput.

DownLoadRateLimitSwitch: If this option is selected, the eNodeB provides a lower-priority guarantee for download services and the scheduling probability decreases. The impact varies as follows:

When the channel quality is favorable and download rates are higher than the maximum bit rate, the decreased scheduling probability reduces the cell throughput.

When the channel quality is unfavorable and download rates are higher than the service maximum bit rate, the decreased scheduling probability improves the cell throughput.

VoiceDataDifferentiationSwitch: If this option is deselected, spectral efficiency increases, but channel reliability for VoLTE services decreases, the packet loss rate increases, and VoLTE experience deteriorates. In the case of concurrent VoLTE and data services, the data service throughput increases. When the UE is extremely close to the cell center in downlink CA scenarios, the UE throughput in the SCell decreases. When the voice packet loss rate reaches the threshold specified by VoltePlrThldForExitingDrx, the throughput of data services increases. If this option is selected, channel reliability for VoLTE services increases, the packet loss rate decreases, VoLTE experience improves, and the service connection delay decreases, but spectral efficiency decreases. In the case of concurrent VoLTE and data services, the data service throughput decreases. When the UE is extremely close to the cell center in downlink CA scenarios, the UE throughput in the SCell increases. When the voice packet loss rate reaches the threshold specified by VoltePlrThldForExitingDrx, the throughput of data services decreases.

AsForTcpSw: If this option is selected, the delay of HTTP- or HTTPS-based TCP services for acceleration UEs decreases. If this option is deselected, the delay of HTTP- or HTTPS-based TCP services for acceleration UEs is not affected.

LowDelayServiceOptSwitch: If this option is selected, the service opening delay is reduced for UEs with low-delay bearers established. If this option is deselected, the service opening delay for UEs with low-delay bearers established is not affected.
