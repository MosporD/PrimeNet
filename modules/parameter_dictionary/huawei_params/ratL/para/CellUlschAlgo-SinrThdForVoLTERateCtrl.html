Meaning

Indicates the SINR threshold for triggering rate adjustment for voice services. If the uplink SINR of VoLTE UEs using the AMR-WB or AMR-NB voice codec is lower than this threshold, the eNodeB lowers the rate of voice services for such UEs, reducing the number of occasions when voice service experience is poor due to continuous packet loss. If this parameter is set to -100, SINR-based rate control for voice services does not take effect. This parameter applies only to LTE FDD and LTE TDD.

Impact on Radio Network Performance

A smaller value of this parameter leads to a lower probability of reducing the rates of uplink voice services, higher PDCCH and RB resource consumption by uplink voice services, and a higher uplink packet loss rate of QCI 1 bearers. A larger value of this parameter leads to the opposite effects.
