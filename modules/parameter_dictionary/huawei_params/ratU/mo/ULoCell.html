Description

The ULOCELL MO configures local cell parameters, such as Local Cell Type, Local Cell Radius, UL Baseband Equipment ID, DL Baseband Equipment ID, UL Frequency Channel Number, DL Frequency Channel Number, Max Output Power, and local cell related switches.
