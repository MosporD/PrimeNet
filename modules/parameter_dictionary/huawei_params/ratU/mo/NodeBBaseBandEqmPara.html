NODEBBASEBANDEQMPARA

+-NE
  +-NODEBFUNCTION
    +-NODEBBASEBANDEQMPARA

Description

The NODEBBASEBANDEQMPARA MO is used to configure NodeB baseband equipment parameters.

Effective Scenario

Immediately

Relations Between MOs

  • Possible parents:

    NODEBFUNCTION
  • Relation with the NODEBFUNCTION MO:
    • The NODEBFUNCTION MO associated with the NODEBBASEBANDEQMPARA MO must have been configured.
  • Relation with the BASEBANDEQM MO:
    • The BASEBANDEQM MO referenced by the BASEBANDEQMID in the NODEBBASEBANDEQMPARA MO must have been configured, and the BASEBANDEQMTYPE in the BASEBANDEQM MO must be UL or ULDL.
    • For the BASEBANDEQM MO referenced by the BASEBANDEQMID in the NODEBBASEBANDEQMPARA MO under which ULBASEBANDEQMWORKMODE is AIR, the UMTSDEMMODE cannot be set to DEM_4_CHAN and DEM_ECON_4_CHAN.
    • In the BASEBANDEQM MO referenced by the BASEBANDEQMID in the NODEBBASEBANDEQMPARA MO under which ULBASEBANDEQMWORKMODE is AIR, the BBP MOs can be configured in only one BASEBANDEQM MO with BASEBANDEQMTYPE set to UL or ULDL.
  • Relation with the BASEBANDEQM and BBP MOs:
    • In a BASEBANDEQM MO referenced by the BASEBANDEQMID in the NODEBBASEBANDEQMPARA MO under which ULBASEBANDEQMWORKMODE is AIR, TYPE of all BBP MOs cannot be WBBP.
  • Relation with the BASEBANDEQM and ULOCELL MOs:
    • If a BASEBANDEQM MO is referenced by the BASEBANDEQMID in the NODEBBASEBANDEQMPARA MO under which ULBASEBANDEQMWORKMODE is AIR, a maximum of six ULOCELL MOs with the ULBASEBANDEQMID referencing the BASEBANDEQM MO can be configured.
    • If a BASEBANDEQM MO is referenced by the BASEBANDEQMID in the NODEBBASEBANDEQMPARA MO under which ULBASEBANDEQMWORKMODE is AIR, LOCELLTYPE for all ULOCELL MOs with the ULBASEBANDEQMID referencing the BASEBANDEQM MO must be NORMAL_CELL.
  • Relation with the NODEBALGPARA MO:
    • If the ULRESWORKMODE in the NODEBALGPARA MO is set to MANUAL_CHAIN or AUTO_CHAIN, the ULBASEBANDEQMWORKMODE in the NODEBBASEBANDEQMPARA MO cannot be set to AIR.
  • Relation with the BASEBANDEQM, ULOCELL, ULOCELLSECTOREQM, and SECTOREQM MOs:
    • When a BASEBANDEQM MO is associated with the NODEBBASEBANDEQMPARA MO under which ULBASEBANDEQMWORKMODE is AIR and UMTSDEMMODE under the BASEBANDEQM MO is DEM_ADPT_CHAN, for each ULOCELL MO configured in the BASEBANDEQM MO, the number of RX antennas configured under the SECTOREQM MOs associated with ULOCELLSECTOREQM MOs under which SECTOREQMPROPERTY is NORMAL must be less than four.
