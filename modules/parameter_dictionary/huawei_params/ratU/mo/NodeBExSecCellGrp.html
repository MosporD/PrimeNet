NODEBEXSECCELLGRP

+-NE
  +-NODEBFUNCTION
    +-NODEBEXSECCELLGRP

Description

The NODEBEXSECCELLGRP MO configures extend secondary cell group parameters, such as Local Cell ID and Extend Secondary Local Cell ID.

Effective Scenario

Immediately

Relations Between MOs

  • Possible parents:

    NODEBFUNCTION
  • Relation with the NODEBMULTICELLGRP and ULOCELL MOs:
    • For a ULOCELL MO, the total number of peer ULOCELL MOs in the NODEBMULTICELLGRP under which MULTICELLGRPTYPE is HSDPA and peer ULOCELL MOs in the NODEBEXSECCELLGRP cannot be greater than 5.
    • For a ULOCELL MO, the total number of peer ULOCELL MOs in the NODEBMULTICELLGRP under which MULTICELLGRPTYPE is HSDPA and peer ULOCELL MOs in the NODEBEXSECCELLGRP cannot be greater than 5.
  • Relation with the ULOCELL MO:
    • The ULOCELL MO referenced by ULOCELLID in a NODEBEXSECCELLGRP MO must have been configured.
    • The ULOCELL MO referenced by EXSECULOCELLID in a NODEBEXSECCELLGRP MO must have been configured.
    • The CELLSCALEIND under the ULOCELL MO referenced by ULOCELLID in a NODEBEXSECCELLGRP MO cannot be set to MICRO.
    • The CELLSCALEIND under the ULOCELL MO referenced by EXSECULOCELLID in a NODEBEXSECCELLGRP MO cannot be set to MICRO.
    • The value of DLFREQ under the ULOCELL MO referenced by ULOCELLID in a NODEBEXSECCELLGRP MO cannot be equal to the value of DLFREQ under the ULOCELL MO referenced by EXSECULOCELLID in the NODEBEXSECCELLGRP MO.
    • The value of DLBASEBANDEQMID under the ULOCELL MO referenced by ULOCELLID in a NODEBEXSECCELLGRP MO must be equal to the value of DLBASEBANDEQMID under the ULOCELL MO referenced by EXSECULOCELLID in the NODEBEXSECCELLGRP MO.
    • In one NODEBEXSECCELLGRP, ULOCELLID and EXSECULOCELLID cannot be corresponding to a cell enabled with Dual Carriers over Narrowband and its neighboring frequency cell in a cell pair.
  • Relation with the NODEBMULTICELLGRP MO:
    • The ULOCELLID under the NODEBEXSECCELLGRP MO must have been configured in a NODEBMULTICELLGRP MO under which MULTICELLGRPTYPE is HSDPA.
    • The ULOCELLID and EXSECULOCELLID under a NODEBEXSECCELLGRP MO cannot be configured in the same NODEBMULTICELLGRP MO under which MULTICELLGRPTYPE is HSDPA.

Function

Use this command to remove an extend secondary cell group.
