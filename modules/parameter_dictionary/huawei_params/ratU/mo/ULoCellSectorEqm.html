ULOCELLSECTOREQM

+-NE
  +-NODEBFUNCTION
    +-ULOCELL

Description

The ULOCELLSECTOREQM MO configures the sector equipment used by the local and the cell power of the sector equipment, such as Sector Equipment ID and Max Output Power.

Effective Scenario

Immediately
