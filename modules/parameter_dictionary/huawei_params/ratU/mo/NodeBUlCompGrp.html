NODEBULCOMPGRP

+-NE
  +-NODEBFUNCTION
    +-NODEBULCOMPGRP

Description

The NODEBULCOMPGRP MO configures ULCoMP group parameters, such as Host Local Cell ID, Neighbour Local Cell ID, and Neighbour NodeB ID.

Effective Scenario

Immediately

Relations Between MOs

  • Possible parents:

    NODEBFUNCTION
  • Relation with the ULOCELL MO:
    • The HOSTLOCELLID under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO must have been configured.
    • The LOCELLTYPE for all local cells associated with the HOSTLOCELLID can not be MULTIRRU_CELL, DIST_CELL, HALFFREQ_CELL, IMB_CELL or MIXED_MULTIRRU_CELL.
    • The RMTCM under the ULOCELL MO associated with the HOSTLOCELLID must be FALSE and RADIUS cannot be greater than 35 km.
    • If one NEIGHBOURLOCELLID under the MO NODEBULCOMPGRP corresponds to multiple HOSTLOCELLID, ULBASEBANDEQMID used by the MO ULOCELL corresponding to HOSTLOCELLID must be the same.
  • Relation with the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO:
    • When ULCOVEXP under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is TRUE, the number of NEIGHBOURLOCELLID associated with one HOSTLOCELLID must be with a maximum of 3.
    • When ULCOMP under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is TRUE, the number of NEIGHBOURLOCELLID associated with one HOSTLOCELLID must be with a maximum of 11.
    • When ULCOMP under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is TRUE, the number of HOSTLOCELLID associated with one NEIGHBOURLOCELLID must be with a maximum of 11.
    • When ULCOVEXP under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is TRUE, the number of HOSTLOCELLID associated with one NEIGHBOURLOCELLID must be with a maximum of 3.
  • Relation with the ULOCELL and NODEBFUNCTION MOs:
    • When the NODEBID under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is the same as the NODEBID under the NODEBFUNCTION MO, the NEIGHBOURLOCELLID must have been configured.
    • When the NODEBID under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is the same as the NODEBID under the NODEBFUNCTION MO, the LOCELLTYPE for all local cells associated with the NEIGHBOURLOCELLID can not be MULTIRRU_CELL, DIST_CELL, HALFFREQ_CELL or IMB_CELL or MIXED_MULTIRRU_CELL.
    • When the NODEBID under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is the same as the NODEBID under the NODEBFUNCTION MO, the RMTCM for all local cells associated with the NEIGHBOURLOCELLID must be FALSE and RADIUS can not be greater than 35 km.
    • When the NODEBID under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is the same as the NODEBID under the NODEBFUNCTION MO, the ULBASEBANDEQMID for the HOSTLOCELLID and the NEIGHBOURLOCELLID must be the same.
    • When the NODEBID under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is the same as the NODEBID of the NODEBFUNCTION, the DLFREQ of the HOSTLOCELLID and the NEIGHBOURLOCELLID must be the same.
    • When the NODEBID under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is the same as the NODEBID of the NODEBFUNCTION, if the ULFREQIND under the ULOCELL MO referenced by HOSTLOCELLID and the ULFREQIND under the ULOCELL MO referenced by NEIGHBOURLOCELLID are set to TRUE, the ULFREQ of the ULOCELL MOs must be the same.
  • Relation with the NODEBFUNCTION MO:
    • When the NODEBID under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is the same as the NODEBID under the NODEBFUNCTION MO, the HOSTLOCELLID and the NEIGHBOURLOCELLID can not be the same.
    • When ULCOVEXP under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is TRUE, the NODEBID under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO and NODEBID under the NODEBFUNCTION MO must be the same.
  • Relation with the ULOCELL and BASEBANDEQM MOs:
    • When ULCOVEXP under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is set to TRUE, UMTSDEMMODE under the BASEBANDEQM MO used by the ULOCELL MO associated with the HOSTLOCELLID must be set to DEM_2_CHAN.
    • When ULCOVEXP under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is TRUE, the following parameter settings must be applied under all the other ULOCELL MOs that belong to the same BASEBANDEQM MO as ULOCELL MO associated with the HOSTLOCELLID: RMTCM cannot be set to TRUE and RADIUS cannot be greater than 35 km.
  • Relation with the ULOCELL, BASEBANDEQM, and BBP MOs:
    • If RADIUS under the ULOCELL MO referenced by HOSTLOCELLID under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is greater than 30 km, TYPE under all BBP MOs configured in the BASEBANDEQM MO referenced by ULBASEBANDEQMID under the ULOCELL MO cannot be WBBP.
    • If ULCOVEXP under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is TRUE, and the BASEBANDEQM MO referenced by ULBASEBANDEQMID under the ULOCELL MO referenced by HOSTLOCELLID is configured with ULOCELL MO under which RADIUS is greater than 30 km, TYPE under all BBP MOs configured in the BASEBANDEQM MO cannot be WBBP.
  • Relation with the ULOCELL, NODEBFUNCTION, BASEBANDEQM, and BBP MOs:
    • If NODEBID under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is the same as NODEBID under the NODEBFUNCTION MO, and RADIUS under the ULOCELL MO referenced by NEIGHBOURLOCELLID is greater than 30 km, TYPE under all BBP MOs configured in the BASEBANDEQM MO referenced by ULBASEBANDEQMID under the ULOCELL MO cannot be WBBP.
  • Relation with the ULOCELL, NODEBFUNCTION, BASEBANDEQM, ULOCELLSECTOREQM, and SECTOREQM MOs:
    • When the NODEBID under the NODEBULCOMPGRP MO is the same as the NODEBID under the NODEBFUNCTION MO, and UMTSDEMMODE under the BASEBANDEQM MOs used by the ULOCELL MOs corresponding to the HOSTLOCELLID and NEIGHBOURLOCELLID is DEM_ADPT_CHAN, for the local cells corresponding to HOSTLOCELLID and NEIGHBOURLOCELLID, the number of RX antennas configured under the SECTOREQM MOs associated with ULOCELLSECTOREQM MOs under which SECTOREQMPROPERTY is NORMAL must be either smaller than 4 or equal to 4.

Related MML Commands

Table 1 describes the MML commands related to this MO.

Table 1 Related MML commands

MML Command

Function
ADD NODEBULCOMPGRP

Use this command to configure the coordination relationship for the Uplink CoMP (Joint Reception) based on Coordinated BBU feature or the Coverage Expansion Under Interference feature. The host cell (primary cell) and neighboring cell (coordinating cell) in the coordination relationship combine received signals, thereby improving uplink performance.
DSP NODEBULCOMPGRP

Use this command to query the status of the coordination relationship for the Uplink CoMP (Joint Reception) based on Coordinated BBU feature and the Coverage Expansion Under Interference feature.
LST NODEBULCOMPGRP

Use this command to query the configuration of the coordination relationship for the Uplink CoMP (Joint Reception) based on Coordinated BBU feature and the Coverage Expansion Under Interference feature.
RMV NODEBULCOMPGRP

Use this command to remove the specified coordination relationship for the Uplink CoMP (Joint Reception) based on Coordinated BBU feature and the Coverage Expansion Under Interference feature.

Related Parameters

Table 2 and Table 3 describes the parameters related to this MO.

Table 2 Configuration parameters

Parameter

Description
Host Local Cell ID

Indicates the ID of the host cell where Uplink CoMP (Joint Reception) based on Coordinated BBU is enabled.
Neighbour Local Cell ID

Indicates the ID of the neighboring cell where Uplink CoMP (Joint Reception) based on Coordinated BBU is enabled.
Neighbour NodeB ID

Indicates the ID of the NodeB serving the neighboring cell where Uplink CoMP (Joint Reception) based on Coordinated BBU is enabled.
ULCoMP Neighbour Cell Configure

Indicates whether to configure the coordination relationship for the Uplink CoMP (Joint Reception) based on Coordinated BBU feature. If this parameter is set to TRUE, the coordination relationship configured using the ADD NODEBULCOMPGRP command is used for the Uplink CoMP (Joint Reception) based on Coordinated BBU feature.
Coverage Expansion Neighbour Cell Configure

Indicates whether to configure the coordination relationship for the Coverage Expansion Under Interference feature. If this parameter is set to TRUE, the coordination relationship configured using the ADD NODEBULCOMPGRP command is used for the Coverage Expansion Under Interference feature.
Table 3 Dynamic parameters

Parameter

Description
ULCoMP Status

Indicates the status of Uplink CoMP (Joint Reception) based on Coordinated BBU.Value range:ULCoMP Available, ULCoMP Unavailable-Local Cell Disabled, ULCoMP Unavailable-UL Baseband Resource Limited, ULCoMP Unavailable-DL Baseband Resource Limited, ULCoMP Unavailable-License Limited, ULCoMP Unavailable-Neighboring Cell Not Supported, ULCoMP Not Configured.
Coverage Expansion Status

Indicates the status of Coverage Expansion Under Interference. Value range:ULCOVEXP Available, ULCOVEXP Unavailable, ULCOVEXP Not Configured.
