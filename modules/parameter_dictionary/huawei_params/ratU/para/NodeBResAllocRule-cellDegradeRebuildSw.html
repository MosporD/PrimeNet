Meaning

Indicates whether to enable immediate reestablishment in the case of cell derating. SPECTRUMFEAORFUNCTION(Spectrum-related Features or Functions) indicates that all cells under the base station are reestablished if any of the cells experiences derating of spectrum-related features or functions and the remaining resources of the base station are sufficient. Spectrum-related features or functions are as follows:

WRFD-191209 GU@5 MHz Phase 1

WRFD-200201 GU@5 MHz Phase 2

WRFD-210203 DC-HSDPA Shared with GSM

MRFD-130212 UMTS and LTE Spectrum Sharing (UMTS)

MRFD-150212 UMTS and LTE Spectrum Sharing Phase 2 (UMTS)

MRFD-150211 UMTS and LTE Zero Bufferzone (UMTS)

MRFD-151212 UMTS and LTE Spectrum Sharing Based on DC-HSDPA (UMTS)

WRFD-191212 Dual Carriers over Narrowband

GU 0 Buffer Zone

Impact on Radio Network Performance

When SPECTRUMFEAORFUNCTION(Spectrum-related Features or Functions) is selected, all cells under the base station are reestablished if any of the cells experiences derating of spectrum-related features or functions and the remaining resources of the base station are sufficient. In this case, all services of the base station are interrupted.
