Impact on Radio Network Performance

A shorter period specified by this parameter indicates higher frequency of beam adjustment, more significant gains in capacity, but higher risks of KPI deterioration. A longer period specified by this parameter indicates lower frequency of beam adjustment, lower risks of KPI deterioration, but less significant gains in capacity.
