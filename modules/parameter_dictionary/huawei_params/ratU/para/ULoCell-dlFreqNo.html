Meaning

Indicates the downlink frequencies channel number of the local cell. The downlink and uplink frequencies channel number of the local cell must stay within the same frequency band. Frequency(MHZ) = (Frequency Channel Number / 5) + Offset

Parameter Relationship

-Configuration constraints:

  • In the ULOCELL MO, the value of DLFREQ must be within the permissible range.
  • In the ULOCELL MO under which ULFREQIND is set to TRUE, the relationships between ULFREQ and DLFREQ must meet the requirements specified by protocols when special uplink and downlink frequencies are used.
  • In the ULOCELL MO under which ULFREQIND is set to TRUE, the relationships between ULFREQ and DLFREQ must meet the requirements specified by protocols when no special uplink and downlink frequencies are used.

-Others:

  • The UL and DL UARFCNs of the local cell must be in the same frequency band.
  • The UARFCNs of the local cell must be the same as that of the logical cell carried on it.
  • Different cells in a sector must use unique UARFCNs.
