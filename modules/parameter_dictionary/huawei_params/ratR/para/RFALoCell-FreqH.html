Meaning

Indicates the downlink end frequency of the RFA cell. The setting must match the signal source and comply with 3GPP specifications. The downlink end frequency must be equal to or smaller than the downlink maximum frequency of the frequency band or frequency channel, and bigger than the downlink minimum frequency of the frequency band or frequency channel. If this parameter is set to 0, the setting is invalid.
