Meaning

Indicates the frequency used by an RFA cell. The setting must match the signal source. In mixed mode operation, downlink frequencies refer to non-NB-IoT frequencies. In MO mode, downlink frequencies refer to NB-IoT frequencies. The cell frequency setting must comply with 3GPP specifications.
