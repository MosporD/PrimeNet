Meaning

Indicates the downlink start frequency of the RFA cell. The setting must match the signal source and comply with 3GPP specifications. The downlink start frequency must be equal to or bigger than the downlink minimum frequency of the frequency band or frequency channel, and smaller than the downlink maximum frequency of the frequency band or frequency channel. If this parameter is set to 0, the setting is invalid.
