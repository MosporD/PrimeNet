Meaning

This parameter specifies the timeslot structure. This parameter applies only to the NR_TDD.

SS1: When the ratio of the low frequency downlink timeslot to the uplink timeslot ratio is 4 to 1, the downlink symbol /GAP symbol/uplink symbol of the S timeslot is 11/1/2, and corresponding parameters are X=2.5ms, x1=3, x2=11, y2=2, and y1=1.
