Meaning

Indicates the cell reselection priority of an E-UTRAN frequency. The value 0 indicates the lowest priority. The value 255 indicates that no reselection priority is configured for the E-UTRAN frequency. For how UEs process the reselection priority of frequencies, see 3GPP TS 38.304.

A UE calculates the sum of this parameter and the cell reselection sub-priority of an E-UTRAN frequency (specified by NRCellEutranNFreq.EutranFreqReselSubPriority) and compares the sum with the cell reselection priority of the serving cell (specified by NRCellReselConfig.CellReselPriority). The target frequency for cell reselection is determined based on the result.

If the sum is greater than the cell reselection priority of the serving cell, the UE initiates a measurement on the neighboring E-UTRAN frequency. If the signal quality of a cell on the neighboring E-UTRAN frequency meets requirements throughout the time specified by NRCellReselConfig.EutranCellReselTimer, the UE starts reselection to that cell. If the sum is smaller than the cell reselection priority of the serving cell, the UE initiates a measurement on the neighboring frequency only when the signal quality of the serving cell is poor. When the signal quality of the serving cell is lower than the threshold specified by NRCellReselConfig.ServFreqLowPriRsrpReselThd throughout the time defined by the E-UTRAN cell reselection timer, the signal quality of a cell on the neighboring frequency is higher than the threshold specified by NRCellEutranNFreq.EutranFreqLowPriReselThld, and the UE has camped on the serving cell for more than 1s, the UE starts cell reselection. The sum of reselection priorities and that of reselection sub-priorities must be different between RATs. For details, see 3GPP TS 38.331.
