Impact on Radio Network Performance

This parameter has an impact on status reporting in AM. A low frequency of status reporting results in a low frequency of status report scheduling but a high probability that the send window size changes to 0, thereby reducing the transmission rate. A high frequency of status reporting results in a high probability of timely acknowledgment to data in the send window and therefore a high probability of rate assurance. However, a high frequency of status reporting results in a high probability of status report scheduling and repeated transmissions, thereby wasting resources.
