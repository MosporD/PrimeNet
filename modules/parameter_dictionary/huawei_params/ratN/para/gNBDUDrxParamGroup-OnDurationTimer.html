Meaning

Indicates the length of the DRX On Duration timer. Because of the impact of CSI reporting intervals and SRS transmission intervals, the actual value of this parameter assigned to a UE may be greater than the configured value. Because of the impact of LongCycle or ShortCycle, the actual value of this parameter assigned to a UE may be smaller than the configured value.

Impact on Radio Network Performance

This parameter, together with the LongCycle, DrxInactivityTimer, ShortCycle, ShortCycleTimer, and DrxRetransTimer parameters, affects the DRX mechanism. If the settings of other DRX parameters remain unchanged, a smaller value of this parameter results in lower UE power consumption but longer service delay, and a larger value of this parameter results in the opposite effects.
