gNBDUNarfcnCgCell

+-gNodeBFunction
  +-gNBDUNarfcnCellGroup
    +-gNBDUNarfcnCgCell

Description

A gNBDUNarfcnCgCell MO defines cells in the gNodeB DU co-NARFCN cell group.

Effective Scenario

Immediately

Relations Between MOs

  • Possible parents:

    gNBDUNarfcnCellGroup
  • Relation with the gNBDUNarfcnCellGroup MO:
    • A maximum of six gNBDUNarfcnCgCell MO instances can be configured for a gNBDUNarfcnCellGroup MO.
  • Relation with the NRDUCell MO:
    • The values of DlNarfcn in NRDUCell MOs corresponding to gNBDUNarfcnCgCell MOs in a gNBDUNarfcnCellGroup MO must be the same.
    • NrDuCellNetworkingMode in the NRDUCell MO corresponding to NrDuCellId in the gNBDUNarfcnCgCell MO must be set to NORMAL_CELL.
  • Relation with the NRDUCell and NRDUCellTrp MOs:
    • The NRDUCell MO corresponding to NrDuCellId in the gNBDUNarfcnCgCell MO must already be configured. The values of TxRxMode in all NRDUCellTrp MOs under the NRDUCell MO associated by NrDuCellId must be set to 64T64R or 32T32R.
