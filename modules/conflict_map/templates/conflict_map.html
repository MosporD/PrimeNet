How strictness & risk work

Strictness sets three thresholds (see API field filters.strictness_profiles for values): max link length (dist_max_km), distance cap for High when both cells are aligned (dist_high_km), and the max azimuth–bearing difference in degrees to count as aligned (az_near_deg).

Risk filters which tier to draw: High / Medium / Low, derived from those rules. To change what each strictness level shows, edit CONFLICT_STRICTNESS_PROFILES in modules/conflict_map/logic.py and refresh the conflict cache.
